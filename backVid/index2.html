Our Mission

Enable flight for everyone.

  1. Prove the capabilities of small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).
  2. Iteratively increase their sizes to safely carry passengers and cargo, piloting them as simply as cars.

We are developing an app called Similarize to help more people collaborate on this mission. Check it out at similarize.com.

Interactive UAV Flight Demo

Explore a 3D simulation of Earth with orbiting UAVs, symbolizing how accessible flight will make the world smaller and more connected, allowing everyone to access every location beyond local boundaries.
