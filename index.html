The gap is the story

The world is building passenger-class eVTOLs. We are in the shop with a skeleton airframe. If you want to help close that gap, you’re invited.

The aim

Where this is going

  1. 1

    Improve small UAVs iteratively until they can be trusted as safe, quiet, capable package delivery vehicles.

  2. 2

    Then increase size so they can carry passengers and cargo and be piloted as simply as cars.

Autonomous cars are a good hint at the end goal of Flight Enabled: making travel less of a pain, so that people and things are easier to bring together. We are not building robotaxis. Self-driving cars show where this is going; flight is the same idea in the air, so people and cargo can meet without the trip being the hard part.

Enabling flight for everyone should involve everyone.
